# Rahisisha — User Stories

Stories are grouped by role and cover the four core journeys : owner setup
& publishing, customer purchase, service quote, and owner monitoring.

---

## Owner

1. As an **owner**, I want to sign up with my phone number and business name and get
an instant online shop URL, so that I can start selling without knowing how to build a
website.

2. As an **owner**, I want to set my logo, brand colours, tone of voice, working hours,
delivery areas and return rules once, so that the AI assistant can answer customer
questions about my policies accurately without me being available.

3. As an **owner**, I want to upload a real product photo and have the AI enhance it in
my brand colours and style  with price, name and logo added as real text layers so that my listings look professional without hiring a designer every week.

4. As an **owner**, I want to review and approve every AI-enhanced photo and caption
before anything goes public, so that nothing appears on my shop or my customers'
WhatsApp that I haven't seen and signed off on first.

5. As an **owner**, I want approved products to be announced automatically on WhatsApp
to my opted-in customers, so that my audience hears about new arrivals without me
composing and sending each message manually.

6. As an **owner**, I want to add product variants (size, colour, measurements) each with
their own stock count, so that customers can pick exactly what they want and I never
accidentally oversell a specific size.

7. As an **owner**, I want M-Pesa payments to land in my wallet and every transaction to
appear in a running statement, so that I always know exactly how much I've earned and
when.

8. As an **owner**, I want to see today's sales, revenue, orders to pack, low-stock alerts,
and an AI-written plain-language summary on my dashboard, so that I understand my
business at a glance without running reports manually.

9. As an **owner**, I want to ask my dashboard free-text questions like "how many blue
dresses are left?" and get an instant accurate answer, so that I can check stock and
sales without digging through tables or calling anyone.

10. As an **owner** running a service business (like photography), I want the AI to collect
job details from an enquiry, send a quote from my preset pricing automatically for
standard jobs, and flag custom jobs for my approval before a quote goes out, so that
I respond quickly without being available at all hours and without giving away custom
pricing without thinking.

---

## Customer

11. As a **customer**, I want to browse a shop's products on my phone without downloading
an app, so that I can see what's in stock before deciding to buy.

12. As a **customer**, I want to ask the shop's AI assistant about price, size, fit, stock and
delivery and get instant accurate answers, so that I don't have to wait for the owner to
reply and move on to a faster seller.

13. As a **customer**, I want to select my size and colour and pay via M-Pesa with a
single STK PIN prompt, so that checkout feels as fast and familiar as any other
M-Pesa payment I make.

14. As a **customer**, I want to receive a WhatsApp confirmation the moment my payment
goes through, so that I know my order is confirmed without having to follow up.

15. As a **customer**, I want to rate my purchase after it is delivered, so that I can share
my experience and help the business owner know how they're doing.

---

## Platform admin

16. As the **platform admin**, I want each business's data products, orders, customers,
messages to be completely invisible to every other business, so that no owner can
ever accidentally or deliberately access another owner's information.

17. As the **platform admin**, I want to see how many AI messages each tenant has used
this month against their plan limit, so that I can enforce usage caps and prompt
upgrades before a tenant runs out mid-month.

18. As the **platform admin**, I want to suspend a tenant account when needed and have
their login blocked immediately, so that I can act quickly if there is a policy violation or
payment issue.
