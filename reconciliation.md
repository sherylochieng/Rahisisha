# Reconciliation (M-Pesa payments)

If a callback never arrives, the order stays unpaid and the attempt stays `pending`, because Safaricom sends each callback once and does not try again, so the customer may have paid and our system would not know. If the same callback arrives twice, the second one changes nothing: the attempt is no longer `pending`, and the database also refuses a repeated M-Pesa receipt, so the shop is credited once. A nightly reconciliation job would find every attempt still `pending` after about 15 minutes, ask Safaricom for its real status with the STK Query, and then credit the wallet or mark the attempt failed using the same safe steps as the callback. It would also list any payment we hold a receipt for but could not apply, such as one that came after the order was cancelled, so a human can refund it. This means a lost or late callback delays the money but never loses it, because the attempt row was saved before the customer was asked to pay. Eventual consistency means the books may be wrong for a short while but are corrected automatically, and it buys us a system that survives dropped messages and repeats instead of one that works only when everything arrives perfectly. The cost is that a shop owner may see "pending" for a few minutes, or until the night run, even though the customer has already paid.
